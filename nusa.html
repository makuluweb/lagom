Getting there

Perth to Denpasar, then a fast boat from Sanur (35 to 45 min). The villa recommends Rocky Fast Cruise (dry jetty landing, best with kids and luggage, departs Sanur 10 am, 12 pm, 2 pm, 5 pm) or Setia Fast Boat (comfiest ride, beach landing, departs 9:30 am, 1:30 pm, 5 pm). Send the boat details to the villa and staff meet you at the harbour.
